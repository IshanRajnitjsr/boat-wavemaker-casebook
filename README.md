# boAt Wavemaker Challenge: Growth and Customer Experience Strategy

A strategy case study for **boAt** (Indian consumer audio and wearables brand) covering customer acquisition, retention and app experience, with financial projections and KPIs for each idea.

## Problem

boAt sells affordable audio and wearables to Millennials and Gen Z in a market growing at over 18% CAGR (2022-2026). The challenge: how can boAt acquire and retain more customers, build a community around the brand and improve customer satisfaction, without losing its affordable, lifestyle positioning?

## What's in this analysis

1. **Consumer journey and persona**: awareness to advocacy, with touchpoints, pain points and fixes for each stage.
2. **Customer and industry analysis**: consumer drivers, market challenges (battery, regulation, privacy, device compatibility) and current target groups.
3. **Acquisition and retention strategy**
   - Acquisition: boAt Music Cruises, Midnight Drops, Party Speaker Rentals
   - Retention: boAt x RedBull Festival, app revamp with push notifications, recycling programme
4. **App UX redesign** with a points-based rewards system.
5. **Community-building ideas**: Spotify partnership ("BoAtHeads Plan"), VR fitness headset, gym partnerships, silent disco nights, social media contest.
6. **Customer satisfaction strategies**, each with KPIs and a cost/revenue estimate:
   - AR Lens billboard campaign
   - Charity-linked limited edition products
   - "Waves" talent and music series

## Selected numbers (all projections and estimates)

| Initiative | Estimate |
| --- | --- |
| AR Lens campaign (month-long) | Cost about Rs 1.26 Cr; projected added order value about Rs 60.45 lakh/day |
| Silent disco night | Cost Rs 9.5 lakh; about 58% projected profit margin |
| Gym partnership reach (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore) | About 16.2 lakh estimated conversions |
| Charity limited edition | Cost about Rs 1.3 Cr; about 600 units/day assumed |

Figures come from the assumptions stated in the deck and are estimates, not actual results.

## KPIs used

- Engagement with the AR lens and conversion of lens codes to purchases
- App downloads driven by campaigns
- Funds raised and first-time purchasers (charity edition)
- Views, votes and social engagement (music series)
